#### A look at where we play tennis

Hello Dear Readers

As you may know, I love tennis and making maps! For this post I decided to change up my typical style and make some straightforward static maps with every tennis court in the state of Washington.

Find below four Maps, each a at a larger scale than the previous, showing the locations of tennis courts.

Each dot may represent more than one court and not every tennis court is represented in this data. Approximately 95% are. 

Enjoy! ---- Aaron
